Privacy Policy

ai-utonomic concierge notifications · Last updated: July 24, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the operator of the ai-utonomic concierge notifications program (the “Program”) collects, uses, and protects information in connection with the SMS text-message notifications sent under the Program. The Program is operated by an individual sole proprietor (the “Operator,” “we,” “us”).

What this Program is. ai-utonomic concierge notifications is a self-hosted personal automation. It sends operational SMS alerts to a single recipient — the account owner’s own mobile phone. There is no public sign-up, and messages are never sent to any third party. Messages are sent from the US number +1 206-502-2860.

1. Information We Collect

The Program collects and uses only the minimum information needed to deliver alerts:

  • Mobile phone number. The single recipient mobile number that the owner configures to receive alerts.
  • Message metadata. Delivery information generated by our SMS provider (Twilio) when a message is sent, such as timestamps and delivery status.

We do not collect names, email address books, location data, browsing activity, or any other personal information through the Program.

2. How the Mobile Number Is Provided (Consent)

The Program is a personal automation the owner runs to notify themselves. The recipient mobile number is configured by the owner directly in the automation’s private environment configuration. Providing and configuring that number is the owner’s own act and constitutes consent to receive the Program’s SMS alerts. There is no public or third-party enrollment.

3. How We Use Information

The mobile number is used solely to deliver the owner’s own operational alerts, which are limited to:

  • an owner decision needs attention;
  • work has landed / completed; and
  • the system has reached a stable or idle state.

These messages are event-driven and low in volume; message frequency varies. We do not use the mobile number for marketing or promotional messaging.

4. SMS / Mobile Information Sharing

No sharing or selling of mobile information. We do not sell, rent, or share mobile information (including your mobile phone number) with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes. Mobile opt-in data and consent are never shared with any third party for marketing.

The only processing that involves a third party is transmission of the message through our SMS provider (Twilio) strictly to deliver the alert, and disclosure where required by law. No other sharing occurs.

5. How Information Is Stored and Protected

The recipient mobile number is stored only in the Operator’s private configuration file on infrastructure the Operator controls. It is not published, exported to marketing systems, or added to any distribution list. We retain the number only for as long as the Program is in use, and remove it upon opt-out or when the Program is retired.

6. Opting Out

You can stop messages at any time by replying with any of the following keywords: STOP, CANCEL, QUIT, UNSUBSCRIBE, END, OPTOUT, STOPALL, or REVOKE. For help, reply HELP or INFO, or contact us at the email below. Message & data rates may apply. Message frequency varies.

7. Children’s Privacy

The Program is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13 years of age.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The “Last updated” date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

9. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy or the Program can be sent to support@aipragmatica.com.
