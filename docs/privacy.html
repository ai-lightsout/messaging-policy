Privacy Policy

aiutonomic Ops Alerts · SMS program

This Privacy Policy covers the aiutonomic Ops Alerts SMS program only (text START to +1 206-502-2860 to enroll — see the opt-in page).

What we collect

When you opt in, we (and our SMS provider, Twilio) retain:

  • Your mobile phone number.
  • The timestamp and content of the opt-in (START), opt-out (STOP), and help (HELP) messages you send, and the confirmation replies we send back.

We do not collect any other personal information through this program, and we do not require an account, app, or purchase to participate.

How we use it

Your phone number and opt-in status are used solely to send you operational system alerts for the aiutonomic / ai-lightsout systems (for example, “an owner decision needs you” or “a build landed”) and the double opt-in confirmation, and to honor STOP/HELP requests.

What we do NOT do

We do not share, sell, rent, or trade your mobile opt-in information — including your phone number — with anyone, for any purpose. Mobile opt-in data is used only to run this alerts program. No mobile information is shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

We share information only with our SMS provider (Twilio) strictly to deliver the messages you asked for, and only as required by law.

Opt-out

Reply STOP at any time to stop all messages from this program. Reply START to re-enroll. Reply HELP for help.

Data retention

We retain opt-in/opt-out records for as long as needed to operate the program and to document consent as required by carriers and applicable law, then delete them.
